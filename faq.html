Yes. The emerging Regulation 19 Hinckley & Bosworth Local Plan allocates the site as MARK004 under Policy PS17 for strategic employment.
HBBC's Employment Land Review identifies a need for up to 65.7 hectares of B2/B8 space. The 2025 Leicester & Leicestershire Strategic Distribution Study places Junction 22 in Area of Opportunity 4 and identifies a residual need of 168,109 sqm at J22 on top of existing commitments.
The latest traffic modelling shows the network is sufficient to accommodate the development. 79% of site traffic is expected to route to the M1, away from local roads. LCC Highways and National Highways have raised no objection.
The landscape assessment identifies that key views towards Billa Barra Hill and Bardon Hill would remain. The site would become increasingly screened as proposed planting matures, with around 17,000 new trees across the site.
Eight attenuation ponds are proposed. These would keep surface water discharge below greenfield rates for a 1-in-100-year storm plus a 40% climate allowance, reducing current run-off by 57% in the western catchment and 20% in the east.
The proposals provide Biodiversity Net Gain for habitats and hedgerows, with wildlife corridors retained and created and long-term management secured. No protected species were found other than badgers, with mitigation plans in place. LCC Ecology, Natural England and HBBC's Tree Officer have raised no objection.
Development noise is assessed at below 35dB against an existing M1 background of 55-63dB. No upward lighting is proposed around the site, and no likely significant effects on air quality have been identified. Environmental Health has raised no objection.
The proposals are expected to support more than 1,000 operational jobs, around 368 construction jobs, £52.1m in estimated annual GVA, £2.2m per year in estimated business rates and around £7.9m of social value, alongside apprenticeships, training and local procurement.
Planning application 25/00931/FUL is currently awaiting determination by Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council. The application was submitted in September 2025.
