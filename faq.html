Frequently asked questions

We hope that this website has provided you with a clear overview of the submitted proposals and the current planning position. Please find below updated answers to some of the main questions.

Need for the development

Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council's Employment Land Review identifies a need for up to 65.7 hectares of B2/B8 employment space. The 2025 Leicester & Leicestershire Strategic Distribution Study also identifies residual strategic employment need at Junction 22.
The site is strategically located immediately adjacent to Junction 22 of the M1 and the A50, providing direct access to the Strategic Road Network. Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council's supporting evidence identifies Junction 22 as one of the Borough's more favourable locations for strategic employment, and the draft Regulation 19 Local Plan identifies the site as a proposed strategic employment allocation MARK004 under Policy PS17.

Environment & Sustainability

Yes. The proposals include Biodiversity Net Gain, retention and creation of wildlife corridors, around 17,000 new trees and long-term habitat management. LCC Ecology, Natural England and Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council's Tree Officer have raised no objection on the matters within their remits.
Eight attenuation ponds are proposed. Surface-water discharge would remain below greenfield rates for the design storm plus climate allowance, with current run-off reduced by 57% in the western catchment and 20% in the east.
Around 17,000 new trees are proposed, alongside extensive woodland, hedgerow and habitat planting. National Forest planting requirements are met and the development would become increasingly screened as planting matures.
Development noise is assessed below 35dB against an existing M1 background of 55-63dB. No upward lighting is proposed and no likely significant effects on air quality have been identified. Environmental Health has raised no objection.

Traffic, Access & Highways

The site benefits from direct access to the M1, A50 and wider Strategic Road Network. The proposals include an upgrade to the Whitwick Road / A50 junction and a new signalised toucan crossing on the A50.
The latest traffic modelling shows the network is sufficient to accommodate the development. Around 79% of site traffic is expected to route to the M1, away from local roads. LCC Highways and National Highways have raised no objection.
The proposals include improved pedestrian and cycle connections, a new signalised toucan crossing over the A50, EV charging and measures to support sustainable travel by future employees.

The Proposals & Design

The submitted application proposes around 64,800 sqm of industrial and logistics floorspace across six units, together with access, landscaping, drainage, ecology and sustainability measures.
The development is expected to support more than 1,000 operational jobs, 368 construction jobs, £52.1m in estimated annual GVA, around £2.2m per year in business rates and around £7.9m in social value.
Planning application 25/00931/FUL was submitted in September 2025 and is currently awaiting determination by Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council.
