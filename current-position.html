Current Position

September 2026 update on planning application 25/00931/FUL.

Planning policy position

Proposed strategic employment allocation

The draft Regulation 19 Local Plan identifies the site as proposed allocation MARK004 under Policy PS17 for strategic employment.

Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council's Employment Land Review identifies a need for up to 65.7 hectares of B2/B8 employment space. The Leicester & Leicestershire Strategic Distribution Study identifies Junction 22 within Area of Opportunity 4 and a residual need at the junction above existing commitments.

HBBC's supporting evidence identifies Junction 22 as one of the Borough's more favourable locations for strategic employment because of its relationship with the Strategic Road Network and access to the labour market.

Policy PS17 sets out requirements around access and highways mitigation, active and sustainable travel, landscape-led design, flood risk and drainage, green infrastructure, utilities and archaeology. These matters have been considered through the application and the technical work undertaken during determination.

The Markfield Neighbourhood Plan became out of date on 10 September 2026, while the scheme continues to respond to its relevant countryside, landscape and habitat policies.

Response to points raised

The latest technical position

Traffic & highways

79% of site traffic routes to the M1. Traffic modelling shows the network can accommodate the development, including the Whitwick Road / A50 junction upgrade and new toucan crossing.

No objection: LCC Highways & National Highways

Landscape

Around 17,000 new trees are proposed. Key views from Altar Stones towards Billa Barra Hill and Bardon Hill would remain, with screening increasing as planting matures.

No major adverse impacts: HBBC Landscape

Flooding & drainage

Eight attenuation ponds would keep discharge below greenfield rates, reducing current run-off by 57% in the western catchment and 20% in the east.

No objection: LLFA, Severn Trent & HBBC Drainage

Noise, light & air

Development noise is assessed below 35dB against the existing M1 background of 55-63dB. No upward lighting is proposed and no likely significant air-quality effects are identified.

No objection: Environmental Health

Ecology

HBBC's supporting evidence identifies environmental sensitivities on parts of the site. The proposals respond through habitat and hedgerow management, retained and new wildlife corridors and Biodiversity Net Gain.

No objection: LCC Ecology, Natural England & HBBC Tree Officer

Heritage

No listed buildings, scheduled monuments or conservation area are located on the site. Geophysics has been undertaken and 4% trial trenching agreed.

Scope agreed: County Archaeology Officer

Local benefits

More than 1,000 operational jobs, 368 construction jobs, £52.1m estimated annual GVA and £7.9m social value, alongside training and local procurement.

Economic benefits

Consultation

2,262 consultation website views, 520 notification letters and direct engagement with the four closest properties. 375 households commented on the Planning Portal.
