The proposals

The full planning application proposes around 64,800 sqm of high-quality industrial and logistics floorspace across six units, together with landscape, drainage, access and sustainability measures.

High-quality employment space

The proposals provide a mix of industrial and logistics space designed to accommodate a range of occupiers and support economic growth in Hinckley & Bosworth and the wider Leicestershire economy.

  • Around 64,800 sqm across six units
  • Over 1,000 jobs once operational
  • Estimated £52.1m annual GVA
  • Estimated £2.2m pa in business rates

Sustainable design

The scheme targets high sustainability standards in construction and operation and incorporates energy-efficient building design.

  • BREEAM Excellent target
  • EV charging provision
  • Air-source heat pumps and efficient systems
  • PV-ready roofs with provision for battery storage

Landscape & ecology

The landscape framework includes extensive planting and habitat measures designed to integrate the development with its surroundings.

  • c.17,000 new trees
  • National Forest planting requirements met
  • Wildlife corridors retained and created
  • Biodiversity Net Gain secured through long-term management

Access & highways

Access has been designed around the site's direct relationship with the M1 and A50. The latest modelling shows the network can accommodate the development.

  • 79% of site traffic routes to the M1
  • Whitwick Road / A50 junction upgrade
  • New signalised toucan crossing on the A50
  • Improved pedestrian and cycle connections
