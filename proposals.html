Our Proposals

Caddick are bringing forward proposals for a sustainable new logistics and employment hub which would support more than 1,000 jobs once operational and provide opportunities for new and existing local businesses.

Indicative CGI - M1 Junction 22

High-quality industrial units

The submitted scheme provides around 64,800 sqm of high-quality industrial and logistics floorspace across six units, creating a new employment hub to support economic growth in the region.

The proposals would support more than 1,000 jobs once operational, generate an estimated £52.1m in annual GVA and around £2.2m per year in business rates.

Current positionThe site is allocated as MARK004 under Policy PS17 in the emerging Regulation 19 Local Plan for strategic employment.

Sustainable in construction and operation

Sustainability remains at the heart of the proposals, with a target of BREEAM Excellent and sustainable building practices integrated throughout construction and operation.

The proposals include EV charging facilities, air-source heat pumps, energy-efficient systems and PV-ready roofs with provision for battery storage.

Design approach

BREEAM Excellent

Energy-efficient buildings, EV charging, low-carbon heating and provision for renewable energy.

Protecting the environment

A substantial part of the site would be dedicated to landscaping, open space and habitat creation. Around 17,000 new trees are proposed and National Forest planting requirements are met.

Eight attenuation ponds would manage surface water and keep discharge below greenfield rates for the design storm plus climate allowance. Biodiversity Net Gain would be secured through long-term management, with wildlife corridors retained and created.

Current positionNo major adverse impacts identified by HBBC Landscape. No objection from the LLFA, Severn Trent, HBBC Drainage, LCC Ecology, Natural England and HBBC's Tree Officer on their respective matters.
Indicative CGI

Traffic and access

The site benefits from direct access to the M1, A50 and wider Strategic Road Network. Traffic modelling shows the network is sufficient to accommodate the development, with 79% of site traffic routing to the M1 away from local roads.

The Whitwick Road / A50 junction would be upgraded to DMRB standard and a new toucan crossing provided on the A50, alongside improved pedestrian and cycle connections.

Current positionLCC Highways and National Highways have raised no objection.
