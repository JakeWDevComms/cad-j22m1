The Site

The site is located immediately adjacent to Junction 22 of the M1 and the A50 at Cliffe Hill Farm, Markfield.

The site is bound by the M1 Motorway to the west, Whitwick Road to the east and the A50 to the south, adjacent to the only M1 motorway junction in Hinckley and Bosworth.

Its location provides direct access to the Strategic Road Network while remaining close to local communities and the local workforce. The emerging Regulation 19 Local Plan now allocates the site as MARK004 under Policy PS17 for strategic employment.

Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council's employment evidence identifies a need for up to 65.7 hectares of B2/B8 space. The Leicester & Leicestershire Strategic Distribution Study also places Junction 22 in Area of Opportunity 4 and identifies a residual requirement at the junction above existing commitments.
