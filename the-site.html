The Site

The site is located immediately adjacent to Junction 22 of the M1 and the A50 at Cliffe Hill Farm, Markfield.

The site is bound by the M1 Motorway to the west, Whitwick Road to the east and the A50 to the south, adjacent to the only M1 motorway junction in Hinckley and Bosworth.

Its location provides direct access to the Strategic Road Network, while remaining close to local communities and the local workforce.

Planning Context

The draft Regulation 19 Local Plan identifies the site as a proposed strategic employment allocation, MARK004 under Policy PS17. The Regulation 19 consultation closed on 21 September 2026 and the plan remains at the pre-submission stage.

Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council's employment evidence identifies a need for additional B2 and B8 employment space. The Council's supporting evidence also identifies Junction 22 as one of the Borough's more favourable locations for strategic employment because of its direct access to the Strategic Road Network and the local labour market.
