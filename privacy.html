Privacy notice

This notice explains how personal information may be handled when you use this website or contact the J22 M1 Employment Hub project team.

Who is responsible for your information?

Caddick Group LTD is responsible for personal data processed by Caddick in connection with this website and project. Development Communications Limited (DevComms) manages this microsite and project communications in support of Caddick.

What information may be collected?

If you contact the project team by email, telephone or post, we may receive your name, contact details, the content of your enquiry and any other information you choose to provide.

When you use the website, hosting and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages requested and the time of the request. The website also records your cookie preference.

If optional analytics is enabled in the future, analytics information will only be collected after you have given consent through the cookie banner.

How we use personal information

Personal information may be used to respond to enquiries, manage communications about the planning application, operate and secure the website, and keep appropriate records relating to the project.

Where optional analytics is used, it will be used to understand how the website is used and improve its content. The lawful basis for optional analytics is your consent. Other processing will normally be carried out where it is necessary for legitimate interests in managing the project and responding to enquiries, or where required by law.

Who information may be shared with

Information may be shared where necessary between Caddick, DevComms and professional advisers or suppliers supporting the project and website. We may also disclose information where required by law.

The interactive map uses third-party map and imagery services. When map content is requested, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser information.

How long information is kept

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including project communications, record keeping and any legal or regulatory requirements. The cookie preference used by this website lasts for 180 days unless you change it sooner.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to ask for access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, portability, or to object to certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

Contact and complaints

For a privacy query relating to this microsite, email caddickj22m1@devcomms.co.uk and mark your message as a privacy query.

Caddick Group LTD's postal address is Castlegarth Grange, Scott Lane, Wetherby, LS22 6LH. You can also read Caddick's full corporate privacy notice.

You also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your personal information has been handled.
