Our Proposals

Caddick are pleased to bring forward proposals for a sustainable logistics and employment hub which would provide and support more than 1,000 jobs once operational, as well as creating opportunities for new and existing local businesses.

Indicative Masterplan

Indicative Masterplan — click to view full size

High-Quality Industrial Units

The submitted scheme provides around 64,800 sqm of high-quality industrial and logistics floorspace across six units, creating a new employment hub to support economic growth in the region. The mix of unit sizes is intended to provide opportunities for a range of occupiers, including growing local businesses.

The site would support more than 1,000 jobs once operational, generate an estimated £52.1m in annual GVA and around £2.2m per year in business rates. The site is allocated as MARK004 under Policy PS17 in the emerging Regulation 19 Local Plan for strategic employment.

Sustainable in construction and operation

Sustainability remains at the heart of the proposals, with a target of BREEAM Excellent and sustainable building practices integrated throughout construction and operation.

The proposals include EV charging facilities, air-source heat pumps, energy-efficient systems and PV-ready roofs with provision for battery storage.

Protecting the environment

The proposals include a substantial landscape framework with around 17,000 new trees, woodland, hedgerow planting and habitat creation. National Forest planting requirements are met and key landscape features would be retained and strengthened where possible.

HBBC's supporting evidence identifies environmental sensitivities on parts of the site. The proposals respond through habitat retention and creation, new wildlife corridors, long-term management and Biodiversity Net Gain.

Eight attenuation ponds would manage surface water and keep discharge below greenfield rates for the design storm plus climate allowance.

Current positionHBBC Landscape identifies no major adverse impacts. The LLFA, Severn Trent, HBBC Drainage, LCC Ecology, Natural England and HBBC's Tree Officer have raised no objection on matters within their respective remits.

Traffic and Access

The site benefits from direct access to the M1, A50 and wider Strategic Road Network. HBBC's supporting evidence identifies this connectivity as one of the key reasons Junction 22 is suited to strategic employment development.

The latest traffic modelling shows the network is sufficient to accommodate the development, with around 79% of site traffic expected to route to the M1 away from local roads.

The proposals include an upgrade to the Whitwick Road / A50 junction to DMRB standard, a new signalised toucan crossing on the A50 and improved pedestrian and cycle connections.

Current positionLCC Highways and National Highways have raised no objection.
