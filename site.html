Cliffe Hill Farm, Markfield

The site

The site is immediately adjacent to M1 Junction 22 and the A50, with direct access to the Strategic Road Network and close links to the local workforce.

Location

At the Borough's key motorway junction

The site is bound by the M1 to the west, Whitwick Road to the east and the A50 to the south.

It lies outside the Green Belt and within the wider National Forest area. The emerging Regulation 19 Local Plan allocates the site as MARK004 under Policy PS17 for strategic employment.

The evidence base identifies the location as one of the most favourable sites in the Borough for strategic employment because of its access to the Strategic Road Network and labour market.
