A sustainable new employment hub for

J22 M1

Caddick have submitted a planning application for a sustainable new employment hub adjacent to Junction 22 of the M1, which will create a wide range of new jobs and boost the local economy. This website provides an overview of the submitted proposals and the current planning position.

Welcome

The site provides an opportunity to deliver a high-quality and sustainable employment hub set within an extensive network of green space, creating over 1,000 new jobs and supporting the local economy.

Planning application 25/00931/FUL was submitted in September 2025 and is currently awaiting determination. The latest planning and technical position is set out on the Current Position page.

About Caddick

The Caddick Group, which includes Caddick Developments, Caddick Construction and Moda Living, is one of the UK's largest privately owned property and construction groups. Growing from our northern roots more than 40 years ago, we take pride in being a family business, as well as a proven and responsible developer.

We are committed to being a good neighbour and to constructive dialogue with the local community throughout the planning and construction processes.
