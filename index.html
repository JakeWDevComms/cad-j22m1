Welcome

J22 M1 Employment Hub

Caddick submitted a full planning application to Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council in September 2025 for a sustainable new employment hub at Cliffe Hill Farm, Markfield.

The submitted scheme provides around 64,800 sqm of industrial and logistics floorspace across six units, set within an extensive landscape framework. It would support more than 1,000 jobs once operational and is now awaiting determination.

Since submission, the application has progressed through detailed technical assessment and consultation. The emerging Regulation 19 Local Plan now allocates the site as MARK004 under Policy PS17 for strategic employment.

Current position

The application is awaiting determination

The latest technical work addresses the principal matters raised during the determination process, including highways, landscape, drainage, ecology, heritage, noise, lighting and air quality.

LCC Highways and National Highways have raised no objection, alongside no objections from the LLFA, Severn Trent, HBBC Drainage, LCC Ecology, Natural England, HBBC's Tree Officer and Environmental Health on the matters within their respective remits.
