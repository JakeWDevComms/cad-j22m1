J22 M1 Employment Hub

Caddick submitted a full planning application to Hinckley & Bosworth Borough Council in September 2025 for a new industrial and logistics development at Cliffe Hill Farm, Markfield.

The site sits immediately adjacent to M1 Junction 22 and the A50 and would provide around 64,800 sqm of employment floorspace across six units. The application is now awaiting determination.

The proposals would support more than 1,000 jobs once operational and provide a substantial new landscape framework, including around 17,000 new trees.

What has changed since submission?

The site is allocated as MARK004 under Policy PS17 in the emerging Regulation 19 Local Plan for strategic employment. The latest evidence also confirms the identified need for employment space at Junction 22.

Highways, landscape, drainage, ecology, heritage, noise, lighting and air quality matters have all been assessed through the determination process. LCC Highways and National Highways have raised no objection, alongside no objections from the LLFA, Severn Trent, HBBC Drainage, LCC Ecology, Natural England, HBBC's Tree Officer and Environmental Health on the matters within their remits.
